Based on the most recent financial reports and data available for **2024–2025**, here is the ranked list of these companies by size (Revenue and Employees).

> **Note on Comparisons:**
> * **Market Context:** The list ranges from global giants (BT, Vodafone) to building-phase infrastructure projects (nexfibre, APFN).
> * **Structure:** Several companies (Openreach, YouFibre, Cuckoo) are subsidiaries or brands of others on this list. I have ranked them individually where data exists but noted their parentage.
> * **Figures:** Revenue figures are for the UK operations where separable (e.g., Vodafone, Sky) to provide a fair comparison.

### **Tier 1: The National Giants (Billions in Revenue)**

1.  **BT Group**
    * **Revenue:** ~£20.8 Billion
    * **Employees:** ~111,000
    * **Context:** The largest telecom provider in the UK. Includes EE, BT Consumer, BT Business, and Openreach.

2.  **Virgin Media O2 (VMO2)**
    * **Revenue:** £10.7 Billion
    * **Employees:** ~18,000
    * **Context:** Joint venture between Liberty Global and Telefónica. The second-largest carrier in the UK.

3.  **Sky (UK)**
    * **Revenue:** ~£10.3 Billion
    * **Employees:** ~25,000+
    * **Context:** Owned by Comcast. A massive media and broadband player; its revenue rivals VMO2, though its broadband footprint is smaller than its TV base.

4.  **Vodafone (UK)**
    * **Revenue:** ~£5.5 – £6.0 Billion (UK Estimate)
    * **Employees:** ~9,000 – 10,000 (UK estimate)
    * **Context:** Global revenue is ~€36B, but the UK operating segment typically accounts for roughly 11-15% of the group.

5.  **Openreach**
    * **Revenue:** ~£5 – £6 Billion
    * **Employees:** ~33,000
    * **Context:** **Wholly owned subsidiary of BT Group.** It operates the national network infrastructure used by BT, Sky, TalkTalk, Vodafone, etc.

6.  **Utility Warehouse (Telecom Plus)**
    * **Revenue:** £2.0 Billion
    * **Employees:** ~800 (Direct) + ~68,000 Partners
    * **Context:** A multi-utility provider (Energy, Broadband, Mobile). Their unique "Partner" model keeps direct employee count low relative to their high revenue.

7.  **TalkTalk**
    * **Revenue:** ~£1.5 Billion
    * **Employees:** ~1,700
    * **Context:** Privately held (taken private in 2021). It remains a major budget ISP but has streamlined operations significantly in recent years.

---

### **Tier 2: The Major Altnets & Mid-Market (Over £100m Revenue)**

8.  **CityFibre**
    * **Revenue:** £134 Million (2024)
    * **Employees:** ~2,000
    * **Context:** The UK's largest independent wholesale network. Recently reported its first profitable year and acquired Lit Fibre.

9.  **Zen Internet**
    * **Revenue:** £121 Million
    * **Employees:** ~550 – 600
    * **Context:** The largest independent ISP not owned by a private equity infrastructure fund. Known for premium customer service.

10. **Hyperoptic**
    * **Revenue:** £114 Million
    * **Employees:** ~1,800
    * **Context:** Focused heavily on urban MDU (apartment) connectivity.

11. **Netomnia / YouFibre / Brsk**
    * **Revenue:** ~£110 Million (Annualised Run Rate)
    * **Employees:** ~1,000 (Combined)
    * **Context:** **Netomnia** (builder) and **YouFibre** (ISP) merged with **Brsk** in 2024. The combined group is now one of the fastest-growing Altnets, hitting £27.7m quarterly revenue in late 2024.

---

### **Tier 3: Scaling Altnets & Specialized ISPs (Under £100m Revenue)**

12. **Community Fibre**
    * **Revenue:** £76 Million
    * **Employees:** ~700
    * **Context:** London-focused network. Recently became EBITDA positive.

13. **Glide**
    * **Revenue:** ~£50 – £60 Million (Estimate)
    * **Employees:** ~300 – 400
    * **Context:** Specialises in Student accommodation and Business broadband.

14. **Gigaclear**
    * **Revenue:** £34 Million
    * **Employees:** ~820
    * **Context:** Focused on rural fibre. High employee count relative to revenue due to the complexity of rural civil engineering.

15. **ITS Technology Group**
    * **Revenue:** ~£25 – £30 Million
    * **Employees:** ~150
    * **Context:** Focuses on "Faster Britain" business fibre networks.

16. **FullFibre**
    * **Revenue:** <£20 Million (Estimate)
    * **Employees:** ~200 – 300
    * **Context:** Wholesale network builder operating under the "Fibre Heroes" brand.

17. **Cuckoo**
    * **Revenue:** <£20 Million (UK Estimate)
    * **Employees:** ~100+
    * **Context:** Now the consumer face of the **AllPoints Fibre** group (see below). Note: Do not confuse with "Cuckoo International" (Malaysia) which is much larger.

---

### **Tier 4: Infrastructure & Holding Companies (High Assets, Low/Hidden Revenue)**

These companies are massive infrastructure projects with billions in funding, but their "Revenue" is often internal or low because they are in the build phase or operate as wholesale holding companies.

18. **nexfibre**
    * **Size:** £4.5 Billion Investment
    * **Revenue:** N/A (Startup/Build phase)
    * **Context:** Joint Venture between Liberty Global, Telefónica, and InfraVia. It is building the network for **VMO2** to use. It has passed 2 million premises but has few direct employees (outsourced build).

19. **APFN (AllPoints Fibre Networks)**
    * **Size:** Consolidated consolidation of G.Network, Swish, etc.
    * **Revenue:** Consolidated under Fern Trading accounts.
    * **Context:** A wholesale aggregator owned by Fern Trading. It consolidates networks like Giganet, Swish, and Jurassic Fibre. **Cuckoo** is their primary ISP brand.
